No scattered apps, no guesswork: one plan that connects what you eat, how you train and how you advance.

Goals with a scientific foundation

Your calories, protein, carbs and fats are calculated from published sports-science research — and your health declaration adjusts them before anything is applied.

A training plan made for you

Your trainer builds your weekly plan, and the Automatic Program can propose the training split that fits your days — you always review it before it goes live.

A weekly menu that plans itself

Concrete dishes for every meal and every day, matched to your goals, with one-tap swaps that respect your allergies — and your shopping list builds itself.

Meals analyzed in seconds

Snap a photo of your plate or dictate what you ate: the Coach estimates calories and macros, flags the labels behind the numbers and keeps your day on track.

Your progress, all in one place

Weight, measurements, progress photos, water, streaks and badges — your whole journey at a glance, with a shareable card when you hit a milestone.

A coach and a trainer by your side

AI that does the typing for you

Seven jobs the app does with artificial intelligence, explained plainly: you show it or say it, it does the writing — and nothing is ever saved until you confirm it.

From a photo of your plate

Snap your meal and the AI identifies the foods and estimates calories and macros. Because it is an estimate, you always review and adjust before it counts toward your day.

From your voice

No time to type? Dictate what you ate — “two eggs, rice and grilled chicken” — and the AI turns your words into a logged meal with its values, ready for your confirmation.

From a sentence you type

Prefer writing? Type what you ate in plain words and the AI does the same job: it finds the foods, estimates the amounts and works out the numbers for you to check.

A Coach you can ask — meet Ronbo

The mascot is a real chat: ask about your day, your week or your plan — by text or by voice — and it answers, even out loud. It only reads your data; it never changes anything on its own.

Your body, from your photos

With your consent, the AI estimates your body type from your own progress photos and feeds your body-composition report — guidance to train smarter, never a medical measurement.

What machine is this?

Facing equipment you don't know? Photograph the machine and the AI tells you what it is and which exercise it is for — no more skipping stations or guessing your way through a new gym.

Your scale's report, read for youComing soon

Photograph the body-composition report from your smart-scale app and the AI reads its values straight into your record — no typing. You confirm every number before it is saved.

And one that needs no AI: the barcode scanner

A barcode already carries the manufacturer's exact data, printed on the package. The app reads the number and looks the product up in the official food databases — USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts — taking the label's real values instead of estimating them. No AI, no guesswork, and it works even with the AI features off. Live today.
