Privacy on this website

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This page explains what information the ronbo.fit website handles. It covers this website only — the RONBO FIT app has its own privacy documents, which are not changed by this page.

What this site collects

  • The RONBO.FIT Business form. If you join the early-access list, the site sends us the three details you type — gym name, city and email — so we can write back to you. Nothing is stored in the page itself.
  • Basic technical delivery. This site is served through GitHub Pages, which may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver and protect the site.
  • Nothing else. This website has no accounts, no comments and no checkout.

Cookies today

This website does not set advertising or tracking cookies of its own today, and no advertising code runs on it yet.

Advertising — prepared, not active

RONBO FIT is preparing this site so it can show advertising in the future. No ads are active yet. When advertising is activated, third-party advertising partners may use cookies and similar technologies to show and measure ads, and this page will be updated to name them before or when that happens. The site's ads.txt file will list the authorized sellers at that time.

Your choices

If you wrote to us through the Business form and want your details removed from our inbox, or you have any question about this page, write to ronbo.fit@gmail.com and we will take care of it.
